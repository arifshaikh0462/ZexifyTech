E-Commerce Website

A proper online store with catalogue, cart, checkout and real payment collection — not just DMs and screenshots.

Get a Free Quote See Our Process
Overview

What this service actually gives you

If you're currently taking orders through WhatsApp or Instagram DMs, an e-commerce website turns that into an organised store: customers browse, add to cart, pay online, and you get a clean order list — instead of chasing payment screenshots.

What's Included

Everything covered in this service

Product catalogue

Categories, variants, pricing and stock — organised and easy for customers to browse.

Cart & checkout

A smooth checkout flow built to reduce drop-offs, on mobile especially.

Payment gateway integration

UPI, cards and net banking connected through a trusted payment gateway.

Order management

A simple dashboard to see, confirm and update orders as they come in.

Order notifications

You (and your customer) get notified automatically when an order is placed.

Mobile shopping experience

Most customers will shop from their phone — the store is built for that first.

Workflow

How it works

Step 1 — Catalogue

Products & categories

We organise your product list, images and pricing into a clean catalogue structure.

Step 2 — Design

Store layout

Homepage, product pages and checkout designed around how your customers actually shop.

Step 3 — Integrate

Payments & shipping

Payment gateway and delivery/shipping details are connected and tested.

Step 4 — Launch

Test orders & go live

We run test orders end-to-end before the store goes live to customers.

Who It's For

Is this the right fit for you?

  • You're currently selling only through WhatsApp or Instagram.
  • You want customers to pay online instead of cash-on-delivery only.
  • You have a growing product list that's hard to manage over chat.
  • You want a store that looks credible for repeat and new customers.

FAQ

Common questions

Which payment gateways do you support?

We typically integrate Razorpay or similar providers supporting UPI, cards and net banking.

Can I add or edit products myself later?

Yes — you get access to manage your catalogue, or we can handle updates through our Website Management service.

Do you handle delivery/shipping setup?

We connect shipping details and delivery charges based on how you currently fulfil orders.

Can it generate GST invoices?

Yes, invoice generation can be set up if you're registered under GST.
