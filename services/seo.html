Overview

What this service actually gives you

Ads stop working the moment you stop paying for them. SEO builds up your website's visibility on Google over time, so customers searching for what you offer find you naturally — through search results that keep working long after the work is done.

What's Included

Everything covered in this service

Keyword research

We identify the exact terms your local customers are searching for.

On-page SEO

Page titles, meta descriptions, headings and structure optimised properly.

Technical SEO

Site speed, mobile-friendliness and sitemap fixes that Google actually checks.

Local SEO

Consistent business info across the web, tied in with your Google Business listing.

Content suggestions

Guidance on pages or blog topics that help you rank for buyer-intent searches.

Monthly ranking report

A clear report on where you rank and what improved.

Workflow

How it works

Step 1 — Audit

Review your site

We check your current site's SEO health — speed, structure, and existing rankings.

Step 2 — Research

Keywords & competitors

We identify what your customers search for and how competitors are ranking.

Step 3 — Optimise

On-page & technical fixes

Titles, content structure and technical issues are fixed across the site.

Step 4 — Monitor

Track & report monthly

Rankings and traffic are tracked, with a monthly summary of progress.
