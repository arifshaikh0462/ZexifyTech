Website Management

Your website updated, monitored and kept running — without you having to chase a freelancer every time.

Overview

What this service actually gives you

A website isn't a one-time purchase — it needs updates, security checks and small fixes over time. Website Management means you have one team on call for edits, monitoring and support, so your site stays fast, secure and up to date without it becoming your job.

What's Included

Everything covered in this service

Content & info updates

Price changes, new photos, new services — updated when you need them.

Security & backup monitoring

Regular backups and monitoring so a hack or crash doesn't mean starting over.

Uptime & speed checks

We track if your site goes down or slows down, and fix it.

Bug fixes

Broken forms, layout issues or errors get resolved quickly.

Small design tweaks

Minor layout and styling changes without a full redesign project.

Monthly report

A simple update on what was changed, fixed and monitored.

Workflow

How it works

Step 1 — Audit

Review current site

We check your existing website's speed, security and structure before starting.

Step 2 — Setup

Monitoring in place

Backups, uptime and security monitoring are set up in the background.

Step 3 — Manage

Ongoing updates

You send update requests as they come up — we handle them within the agreed turnaround.

Step 4 — Report

Monthly summary

A short report each month on what was updated, fixed and monitored.

Who It's For

Is this the right fit for you?

  • You already have a website but no one is maintaining it.
  • Your site was built by a freelancer who's no longer reachable.
  • Your site feels slow, outdated, or you're unsure if it's even secure.
  • You don't have time to keep content updated yourself.

FAQ

Common questions

What if my site wasn't built by Zexify?

That's fine — we can review and manage most existing websites, including WordPress and custom-built sites.

How fast are update requests handled?

Standard updates are typically completed within 1–2 working days, depending on your plan.

What counts as a 'small' design tweak vs a redesign?

Text, image, colour and layout adjustments are included. A full visual redesign is scoped separately.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, management plans are month to month with no long-term lock-in.
