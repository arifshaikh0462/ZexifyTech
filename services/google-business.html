Overview

What this service actually gives you

Most customers check Google before visiting or calling a business. If your Google Business Profile is missing, unverified, or incomplete, you're invisible exactly where people are looking. We set it up properly and keep it optimised so you show up in local search and Maps.

What's Included

Everything covered in this service

New profile setup & verification

We create and verify your Google Business Profile from scratch if you don't have one.

Category, hours & photos

Correct business category, accurate hours, and quality photos set up properly.

Service area & description

Optimised description and service areas so you appear for the right local searches.

Review response guidance

Simple guidance on responding to reviews to build trust and ranking.

Posts & offers setup

Regular updates and offers posted to keep your profile active.

Ongoing optimisation

Continuous small improvements based on what's working in local search.

FAQ

Common questions

How long does verification take?

Google's verification process usually takes a few days to two weeks, depending on the method used.

Can you fix a listing that already exists?

Yes — if you already have a profile, we can claim, correct and optimise it.

Does this guarantee the #1 ranking?

No one can guarantee a top ranking, since Google's algorithm changes — but a complete, well-optimised profile significantly improves visibility.

Is this a one-time job or ongoing?

Initial setup is one-time; ongoing optimisation is recommended as a monthly service for best results.
